Your data stays under your control

No PulseDeck account or central backend. Optional integrations contact their own services; settings and encrypted credentials stay on your Windows PC. Gmail reads metadata for unread counts and arrival notifications, without reading message bodies or attachments.

Built around a verified display

Tested with TURZX 8.8″, 1920 × 480, chs_88inch.dev1_rom1.90. Browser animation and USB delivery run independently: about 10 fps in the preview, about 4 fps on the verified panel. Internal video playback is experimental.
