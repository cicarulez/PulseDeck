# Community conduct

PulseDeck welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, skill levels and access to hardware.

Be respectful and constructive. Explain technical disagreements with evidence, assume questions are asked in good faith, and give people room to learn. Focus reviews on the work. Respect privacy and ask before sharing someone else's personal information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing and persistent unwanted contact are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation or ban participants when necessary. Responses should be proportionate, consider context and protect affected people. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other official project spaces.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer through a private contact method listed on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/cicarulez), if available. If no private channel is listed, use GitHub's **Report abuse** action on the relevant content; do not publish sensitive allegations or personal details in an issue.
